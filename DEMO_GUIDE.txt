Script Production Checklist Agent - Ling Yang

1. Show scenes/complete.txt and explain the four required categories.
2. Run: python3 agent.py --scene complete
   Show each model-selected tool, its returned observation, and verified finish.
   Point out EVA, NOAH, COMMUNITY STUDIO, blue folder, table and camera.
3. Show scenes/incomplete.txt. It deliberately omits the setting and names no
   concrete identity for "the item".
4. Run: python3 agent.py --scene incomplete
   Show the empty locations list, the prop uncertainty, and both questions.
   Explain why needs_clarification is a successful handling of a problem case,
   rather than a claim that production details are complete.
5. Run: python3 verify_reports.py
   Explain that separate Python assertions check exact scene evidence, expected
   characters/props, missing location, uncertainty and final question consistency.
6. Run: python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -v
   Show tests that reject an invented prop, invalid scene ID and premature finish.

Questions to prepare for:
- Who decides the next action? The local model. Python validates and executes it.
- Why is this more than a fixed script? The model chooses from two tools and a
  finish action; it extracts scene facts and responds to actual tool observations.
  Its final decision differs between the complete and ambiguous scenes.
- What is in the context? System rules, the request, prior model outputs and tool results.
- Does feedback retrain the model? No. Context changes; model weights do not.
- How is success checked? Quote checks and finish guards during the run, plus
  separate assertions on saved reports and source fixtures.
- What remains uncertain? Semantic completeness and correctness for unseen scenes.
  A human reviews the checklist. Two demonstrations do not prove general reliability.

Classroom explanation:
"My agent prepares a production checklist from a fictional scene. The model
chooses tools, and Python checks the arguments and evidence. In the complete case,
it returns a checklist for human review. In the incomplete case, it identifies a
missing location and an unclear prop, then asks specific questions instead of
inventing the answers."
